RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is a technique in Generative AI that allows an LLM to retrieve relevant information from external data sources before generating an answer.

Why do we need RAG?

Reduces hallucinations: Helps the LLM answer using relevant, retrieved information.

Access to private data: Allows the LLM to answer questions using your PDFs, documents, databases, etc.

Up-to-date information: Provides information that may not be present in the model's training data.

No retraining required: You can update the external knowledge base without retraining the LLM.

Example: Suppose you build a chatbot for your company. Using RAG, it can retrieve information from company documents and answer employees' questions accurately.

In simple terms: RAG = Retrieve relevant information + Give it to the LLM + Generate an answer.
