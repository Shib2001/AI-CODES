In Generative AI (GenAI), prompt styles and prompt serialization styles are related, but they mean different things.
- Prompt styles describe how you instruct an AI model to get the desired output.
- Prompt serialization styles describe how messages, instructions, and metadata are structured and formatted before being passed to the model.
Since you're learning GenAI development, especially the backend side, understanding both concepts is useful when working with LLM APIs, chat models, and open-source models.
